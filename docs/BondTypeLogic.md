```text id="df1i0o"
Coupon Rate > Market Yield  → Premium

Coupon Rate = Market Yield  → Par
```

This is how finance professionals think about bonds conceptually.

# Why This Is Better Finance Logic

Because bond pricing fundamentally depends on:

```text id="6upm93"
Bond Coupon vs Required Market Return
```

---

# Intuition

## Premium Bond

If your bond pays:

```text id="i08d1w"
8% coupon
```

while market yield is:

```text id="r77q2w"
5%
```

your bond is attractive.

Investors will pay MORE than face value.

So:

```text id="t1hqvc"
Price > Par
```

---

## Discount Bond

If your bond pays:

```text id="nb0fcl"
5% coupon
```

while market requires:

```text id="pcfx9h"
8%
```

your bond is unattractive.

Price must fall.

So:

```text id="h5ngh6"
Price < Par
```

---

# Core Bond Market Identity

This relationship is central:

{Coupon Rate} > {Yield} -> {Premium Bond}

and

{Coupon Rate} < {Yield} -> {Discount Bond}
